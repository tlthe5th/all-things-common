## 1. The Critique of Extraction
Wage theft, corporate landlords, and perpetual property tax are not accidents. They are **πλεονεξία** (greed) built into the system. **Θεός** (God) wrote the economic rules first: the land is not sold permanently, debts are released, every family's permanent home **κληρονομία** (inheritance) is protected, and among the believers there was not a needy person.

## 2. Who Wrote the Rules First? — γραφή (Scripture) Foundations
The Greek Scriptures establish four foundational laws against economic extraction:
* **Land Ownership & the Jubilee — Λευιτικόν (Leviticus) 25:10, 23 (LXX):** Affirms that land belongs ultimately to **Θεός** (God) alone (*"for the land is mine, because you are strangers and sojourners before me"*) and establishes a 50-year reset for debts, debt-slavery, and family **κληρονομία** (inheritance).
* **Early Christian Community — Πράξεις (Acts) 4:32, 34–35:** Records the early believers holding **ἅπαντα κοινά** (all things common) and distributing resources to each **καθότι ἄν τις χρείαν εἶχεν** (according as anyone had need), so that **οὐδὲ γὰρ ἐνδεής τις ὑπῆρχεν** (neither was anyone among them lacking).
* **The Mission to the Poor — Λουκᾶς (Luke) 4:18 / Ἠσαΐας (Isaiah) 61:1 (LXX):** *"The πνεῦμα (spirit) of Κύριος (the Lord) is upon me, because of which it anointed me to proclaim good news to the πτωχοί (poor); it has sent me to proclaim ἄφεσις (release) to the captives."*
* **Voluntary vs. Coerced Sharing — Πράξεις (Acts) 5:4 & Ἰωάννης (John) 4:24:** Property sharing in **Πράξεις** (Acts) was strictly voluntary (*"While it remained, did it not remain your own?"*). True **κοινωνία** (fellowship/community) flows from **μετάνοια** (repentance) and **πνεῦμα ἅγιον** (spirit holy)—which is **Θεός** (God) the **Πατήρ** (Father) and **Χριστός** (the Christ) dwelling in the believer—breaking **πλεονεξία** (greed) from the inside out.
