# Two Columns: Modern Manifesto Goals vs. Ancient Greek Scripture

Many people assume that opposition to land hoarding, usury, and wealth extraction began in the 19th century. In reality, the **Septuagint (LXX)** and the **Greek New Testament** established these protections nearly two millennia earlier.

| Core Principle | Modern Manifesto Framing | Ancient Biblical Baseline (LXX & Greek NT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Distribution by Need** | *"From each according to ability, to each according to need."* | **Πράξεις (Acts) 4:35:** Distribution was made to each *"καθότι ἄν τις χρείαν εἶχεν"* ("according as anyone had need"), so that *"οὐδὲ γὰρ ἐνδεής τις ὑπῆρχεν ἐν αὐτοῖς"* ("neither was anyone among them lacking"). |
| **Common Stewardship** | Abolition of private monopolies over essential survival resources. | **Πράξεις (Acts) 4:32:** Not one claimed his possessions as *ἴδιον* (exclusively his own), *"ἀλλ᾽ ἦν αὐτοῖς ἅπαντα κοινά"* ("but all things were common to them"). |
| **Land & Housing Rights** | Ending landlord speculation and permanent land accumulation. | **Λευιτικόν (Leviticus) 25:23 (LXX):** *"καὶ ἡ γῆ οὐ πραθήσεται εἰς βεβαίωσιν· ἐμὴ γάρ ἐστιν ἡ γῆ"* ("And the land shall not be sold permanently, for the land is mine"). Every family holds an un-taxable inheritance (*κληρονομία*). |
| **Debt Cancellation** | Canceling predatory debt bondage that traps working families. | **Λευιτικόν (Leviticus) 25:10 (LXX) & Δευτερονόμιον (Deuteronomy) 15:1–2 (LXX):** Mandatory debt release (*ἄφεσις*) every 7th year and full land/liberty restoration every 50th year (Jubilee). |
| **Anti-Usury & Wage Justice** | Stopping interest extraction and stolen labor value. | **Ἔξοδος (Exodus) 22:25 (LXX) & Ἰάκωβος (James) 5:4:** Prohibits charging interest (*τόκος*) to the poor and condemns landowners who withhold the wages of workers who harvested their fields. |
| **What Drives the System** | State enforcement, material dialectic, and centralized bureaucracy. | **Πράξεις (Acts) 5:4 & Ἰωάννης (John) 4:24:** Strictly voluntary (*"While it remained, did it not remain your own?"*). Driven by internal repentance (*μετάνοια*) and faith (*πίστις*) in **Ἰησοῦς Χριστός**, not state force. |
