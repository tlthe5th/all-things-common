# Open Dialogues: Honest Answers to Materialist & Leftist Objections

This space addresses the most common questions raised by communists, socialists, and materialists when examining the **Septuagint (LXX)** and **Greek New Testament**.

---

### Q1: Didn't Karl Marx prove that religion is the "opium of the people"?
**Answer:** Marx accurately described *counterfeit institutional religion*—systems where wealthy clergy bless wars, hoard land, and tell poor workers to accept exploitation quietly in exchange for a reward after death. 

However, that critique does not apply to **Ἰησοῦς Χριστός (Jesus the Christ)**. He openly condemned religious leaders who devoured widows' houses (**Mark 12:40**), drove money-changers out of the temple (**John 2:15**), and launched His mission by declaring *"good news to the πτωχοί (poor)"* and *"ἄφεσις (release) to the captives"* (**Luke 4:18**). Counterfeit religion sedates the oppressed; authentic faith confronts the oppressor.

---

### Q2: Why can't we build a just communal society through materialism alone without God?
**Answer:** Look at the historical track record of every 20th-century state that tried to enforce sharing without changing the human heart (*καρδία*). When you abolish private landlords by state decree while leaving greed (*πλεονεξία*) and pride intact inside human beings, the state bureaucracy simply becomes the new landlord.

In **Πράξεις (Acts) 5:4**, Peter explicitly affirmed that property sharing among believers was voluntary (*"While it remained, did it not remain your own?"*). Sharing works permanently only when **μετάνοια (repentance)** and faith in **Ἰησοῦς Χριστός** uproot selfishness from the inside out.

---

### Q3: Doesn't Christianity defend capitalism and billionaire wealth?
**Answer:** Modern Western church culture often defends corporate capitalism, but the Greek Scriptures do the exact opposite:
* **1 Τιμόθεος (1 Timothy) 6:10:** *"ῥίζα γὰρ πάντων τῶν κακῶν ἐστιν ἡ φιλαργυρία"* ("For the love of money is a root of all evils").
* **Λουκᾶς (Luke) 16:13:** *"οὐ δύνασθε Θεῷ δουλεύειν καὶ μαμωνᾷ"* ("You are not able to serve God and mammon/wealth").
* **Ἠσαΐας (Isaiah) 5:8 (LXX):** *"οὐαὶ οἱ συνάπτοντες οἰκίαν πρὸς οἰκίαν καὶ ἀγρὸν πρὸς ἀγρὸν ἐγγίζοντες"* ("Woe to those who join house to house and bring field near to field" until there is no room left for anyone else).

Hoarding single-family homes while families sleep on the street is directly condemned in Scripture.

---

### Q4: How do I test these claims for myself without trusting any church or leader?
**Answer:** You are commanded not to trust any human institution blindly. **1 Θεσσαλονικεῖς (1 Thessalonians) 5:21** instructs: *"πάντα δὲ δοκιμάζετε, τὸ καλὸν κατέχετε"* ("Test all things; hold fast to what is good"). 

Read the **Septuagint (LXX)** and the **Greek New Testament** directly, examine the logic (*λόγος*), and verify every claim against primary evidence.

---

### Q5: If love fulfills the law, why do we need any economic reform at all? Isn't love just a personal feeling?
**Answer:** **ἀγάπη** (love) in **γραφή** (scripture) is not a passive feeling—it is concrete action that *"works no κακόν (evil) to the πλησίον (neighbor)"* (**Ῥωμαίους / Romans 13:10**). 

A person who truly loves their neighbor will not deceive, overcharge, hoard homes, or trap families in lifelong debt. Furthermore, **Παῦλος** (Paul) commands speaking **ἀλήθεια** (truth) with our neighbor (**Ἐφεσίους / Ephesians 4:25**), which requires exposing systems that profit from concealment. Love that stays silent while the neighbor is harmed is not **ἀγάπη** (love). Real reform starts inside the **καρδία** (heart) and then shows up visibly in how land, homes, and money are handled.
