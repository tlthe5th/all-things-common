# Contributing to All Things Common (`ἅπαντα κοινά`)

Everyone who wants to help build open, transparent tools for human provision and truth is welcome. No titles, no hierarchy, and no gatekeeping.

## Ways to Contribute
* **Developers:** Improve the Jubilee Extraction Calculator (`calculator.html`) or help build Version 2.0 of the decentralized community needs-ledger.
* **Translators:** Translate `README.md` and the `/scripture` verification pages into Spanish, Russian, French, Arabic, Chinese, or other languages.
* **Researchers:** Submit verified Septuagint (LXX) and Greek New Testament passages, historical mutual-aid records, or local housing vacancy data.
* **Artists & Designers:** Contribute clean, open-source visual graphics to `/art` illustrating how *λόγος* (logic) cuts through left/right division.
* **Writers:** Draft clear, respectful Q&A entries for `/dialogues` addressing materialist and leftist questions.

## How to Submit Changes
1. **Open an Issue:** Click the **Issues** tab at the top of the repository to propose an idea, report an error, or post a local need/surplus.
2. **Fork & Edit:** Fork this repository, make your improvements, and submit a **Pull Request** with a clear summary of what you changed and why.
3. **Keep It Open:** All contributions must remain free and open-source under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**.

## Ground Rules
* **Transparency (*φανερός*):** Show your sources, show your math, and keep all documentation open to public audit.
* **Respect & Truth (*ἀλήθεια*):** Debate ideas rigorously without personal malice or tribalism. This project openly invites people to examine Scripture and come directly to **Ἰησοῦς Χριστός (Jesus the Christ)**.
