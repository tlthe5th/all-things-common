# Koinonia Events (`κοινωνία`): Transparent Event & Music Allocation Model

Modern entertainment monopolies, ticketing middlemen, and record labels routinely extract **80% to 90%** of the revenue generated by independent artists and local workers. 

The **Koinonia Events** model replaces opaque corporate contracts with a **100% open-ledger split** designed to sustain creators, local laborers, and the vulnerable.

---

## The 70 / 15 / 10 / 5 Open-Ledger Split

| Allocation | Share | Purpose & Accountability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Artists & Performers** | **70%** | Paid directly to the musicians, speakers, and creators who bring the work to life—reversing corporate label extraction. |
| **Community Workers** | **15%** | Paid directly to local sound techs, setup crews, security, and event coordinators who run the gathering. |
| **Care for the Poor (*πτωχοί*)** | **10%** | Dedicated directly to local food, emergency shelter, and immediate material needs in the host neighborhood (**Acts 4:35**). |
| **Operations Reserve** | **5%** | Covers venue rental, basic equipment upkeep, and open-source logistics—with every receipt published publicly. |

---

## Core Operational Rules
1. **Zero Hidden Deductions (*φανερός*):** Total ticket/door receipts and exact payouts are posted openly after every event. No "Hollywood accounting" or hidden administrative fees.
2. **Direct-to-Hand Distribution:** Funds for the poor go straight to verified local needs (food, utilities, temporary housing) rather than bloated nonprofit overhead.
3. **Open Blueprint:** Any band, local cell, or community organizer can copy and run this exact model under the **AGPL-3.0** license.
